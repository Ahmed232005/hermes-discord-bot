# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-22

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot handles information.

## Information That May Be Processed

The bot may process information provided through Discord, including:

- Discord user ID
- Username and display name
- Server and channel information
- Messages or commands sent to the bot
- Files or other information intentionally provided to the bot

## How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Operate the bot
- Process user requests
- Execute commands
- Generate responses
- Maintain and troubleshoot the bot

## AI Services

If the bot is configured to use an external AI service, messages or other information necessary to process a request may be sent to that service.

The privacy practices of that service may also apply.

## Storage

Bot configuration and authentication information may be stored locally on the bot owner's computer.

Information may also be processed or stored by third-party services configured by the bot owner.

## Sharing

The bot owner does not intentionally sell personal information.

Information may be sent to third-party services when necessary for the bot's operation, such as AI providers.

## Data Retention

Information is retained only as necessary for operating and maintaining the bot, subject to the policies of any third-party services used.

## Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the bot owner through Discord.
